GFS forward test

Paper trades only. GFS failed its backtest — this page checks whether live trades behave like the backtest. It is not a list of trades to take. Tap any stock to open it in TradingView on your GFS layout.
R = the risk on one trade (entry − stop). +1R = made what was risked, −1R = lost it. · SL = stop hit before target · TRAIL = target touched, then trailing stop hit · TIME = held 40 sessions · ★7 = first pullback after a fresh M/W regime · “added later” = the daily job missed the signal (data outage) and it was filled in afterwards.
